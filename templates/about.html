Our Origin

Kolhapur & Beyond.

Every city has a unique culinary heartbeat and architectural heritage. In Kolhapur, this heartbeat is found in the legendary thalis of Dehati and Parakh, the historic spiciest misals, and the premium resort stays like Sayaji Hotel and The Fern Residency.

Make My Moment was built to eliminate decision fatigue. Instead of endless directories and generic, unverified review sites, our smart recommender matches your specific group size, budget tier, and mood to the finest spots in town.

We built a system that values visual excellence, algorithmic speed, and hospitality accuracy to connect local premium partners with high-intent travelers instantly.
